**Description:**

Version 2.0.0 extends the Jolo Island Surface Heat GeoAI reproducibility package with matched geographic validation of predictive and attribution transferability. SHAP explanations are recalculated from geographically retrained models under spatially blocked and municipality-held-out validation. The release adds multiscale attribution-stability outputs, prediction-versus-attribution transfer diagnostics, environmental-support analyses, block-origin sensitivity, cross-algorithm stress tests, dominant-predictor ablation, seasonal matched explanation stability, random-seed sensitivity, and integrity-verification scripts. The original Version 1 analysis-ready datasets and canonical benchmarks are retained for provenance.
